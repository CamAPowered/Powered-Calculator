Annual totals use standard month lengths (February = 28 days unless leap year is checked). For LADWP, each entry covers a two-month billing period and is multiplied by the combined days of both months. Cost estimates apply this bill's cost per kWh across the whole year, and only appear once both usage and cost are entered. Switching utilities converts entered values to keep the annual total consistent.
